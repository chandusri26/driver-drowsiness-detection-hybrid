3. HYBRID REAL-TIME SYSTEM
---------------------------

Geometric Features:
- Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR)
- Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR)

Deep Learning:
- Eye MobileNetV2 CNN
- Yawn MobileNetV2 CNN

Fusion:
- EAR + Eye CNN
- MAR + Yawn CNN

Temporal Logic:
- Continuous eye closure threshold: 5 seconds
- Continuous eye opening recovery threshold: 3 seconds
- Yawn duration threshold: 1 second

Final Runtime Configuration:
- Resolution: 640 x 480
- CNN inference interval: Every 2 frames
- Average FPS: 3.42


4. DETECTION BEHAVIOR
---------------------

Normal state:
Driver remains in alert state when eyes are open and no
persistent yawn is detected.

Yawning:
Yawn CNN and MAR fusion produces a yawn warning.
Yawning does not directly trigger the drowsiness alarm.

Drowsiness:
The system triggers a drowsiness alert when the hybrid eye
decision remains closed for at least 5 continuous seconds.

Recovery:
The alarm is stopped only after the eyes remain open continuously
for at least 3 seconds.
